# ImageGen prompts — dự án sắp ra mắt

Hai ảnh được tạo bằng công cụ ImageGen tích hợp ngày 04.09.2026 và được xuất sang WebP để dùng trên website.

## Dòng Sông Đức Tin

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: wide editorial illustration for a premium Vietnamese personal landing-page feature card about an upcoming Catholic history encyclopedia app
Primary request: Create a richly layered, cinematic visual for “Dòng Sông Đức Tin”, an app envisioned as an encyclopedia of 2,000 years of Church history.
Scene/backdrop: A luminous river flows from the distant ancient Mediterranean world through a visual sequence of early house-church arches, a Roman basilica, a medieval monastery and Gothic cathedral, then toward a warm contemporary Vietnamese Catholic church silhouette in the near distance. An open illuminated historical atlas rests subtly in the foreground, with the river appearing to flow out of its pages.
Subject: The river is the central metaphor and guides the eye through successive eras of Christian history.
Style/medium: sophisticated painterly digital editorial illustration, historical atmosphere with restrained detail, elegant rather than fantasy, suitable for a serious cultural and educational app.
Composition/framing: horizontal 3:2 landscape, deep perspective, generous calm negative space near the lower edge for adjacent web layout, strong focal river curve, no framed UI and no device mockup.
Lighting/mood: quiet dawn light, contemplative, scholarly, hopeful, small points of warm candlelight.
Color palette: deep midnight navy, muted stone, parchment ivory, antique gold, very restrained burgundy accents; harmonious with a refined literary website.
Constraints: respectful Catholic visual language; architecture should feel historically varied without claiming documentary precision; no legible text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no modern brand marks, no neon, no glossy 3D, no gradients that look like generic app marketing.
Avoid: kitsch religious poster style, crowded collage, photorealistic faces, identifiable saints, floating interface elements, typography.
```

## Những Vì Sao Sáng

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: wide editorial illustration for a premium Vietnamese personal landing-page feature card about an upcoming Catholic storytelling app
Primary request: Create a poetic, luminous visual for “Những Vì Sao Sáng”, an app that tells the stories of patron saints deeply venerated by Vietnamese Catholics.
Scene/backdrop: A serene Vietnamese parish courtyard at blue hour beneath a star-filled sky. An open storybook in the foreground releases a gentle constellation of golden lights; those lights form a graceful gallery of small stained-glass-like saint medallions and subtle saintly silhouettes in the sky, while the warm church windows below suggest living devotion in the community.
Subject: The open book, the constellation of guiding lights, and the relationship between sacred stories and Vietnamese Catholic life.
Style/medium: sophisticated painterly digital editorial illustration, warm human atmosphere, refined sacred-art influence, elegant and contemporary rather than fantasy or devotional kitsch.
Composition/framing: horizontal 3:2 landscape, clear visual path from the book to the stars, balanced negative space, suitable for cropping into a website feature card, no framed UI and no device mockup.
Lighting/mood: intimate blue hour, candle warmth, contemplative, tender and hopeful.
Color palette: deep oxblood, indigo night, muted parchment, antique gold, warm ivory; visually paired with a refined navy-and-gold companion illustration.
Constraints: respectful Catholic visual language; saint figures remain symbolic and non-specific because no individual saints were named; subtle Vietnamese architectural and landscape cues; no legible text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no halos on ordinary people, no neon, no glossy 3D.
Avoid: crowded celestial collage, photorealistic faces, horror-like figures, generic Christmas imagery, cheesy sparkle effects, typography, app interface elements.
```
